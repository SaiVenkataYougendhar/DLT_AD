# ABSTRACT

## Title: RadCortex-AI: Deep Learning with Quantitative Radiomics for Multi-Class Brain Tumor Diagnosis and Prognostic Treatment Planning

---

### Unstructured Academic Abstract

Intracranial neoplasms—predominantly gliomas, meningiomas, and pituitary adenomas—represent one of the most critical diagnostic and therapeutic challenges in contemporary neuro-oncology, demanding rapid, accurate, and reproducible radiological interpretation. Conventional clinical workflows rely on manual visual assessment of cranial Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) scans, a process subject to cognitive fatigue, inter-observer diagnostic variability, and prolonged turnaround times that can defer time-sensitive interventions. While contemporary deep learning systems demonstrate high nominal classification accuracy, existing computer vision approaches predominantly operate as opaque "black-box" models. They lack quantitative radiological biomarker grounding, omit clinical quality control gates (frequently misclassifying out-of-distribution non-medical graphics as healthy anatomy), and fail to bridge diagnostic inference with actionable prognostic planning such as surgical resectability, radiation sensitivity, and chemotherapy responsiveness.

To address these translational deficiencies, this project presents **RadCortex-AI**, an end-to-end clinical decision support platform that synergistically couples Convolutional Deep Learning with Image Biomarker Standardisation Initiative (IBSI) compliant Quantitative Radiomics. The system introduces a five-stage automated Cranial MRI Quality Control Gate that interrogates upload monochromaticity, dark scanner air boundaries, line-art density, and cranial vault morphometry before tensor dispatch, reliably rejecting non-cranial graphics, flowcharts, documents, and natural images with $0.0\%$ confidence to eliminate erroneous clinical reporting. For verified cranial scans, the platform executes dual-scale Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) through an EfficientNet-B0 backbone featuring 16 inverted residual blocks and squeeze-and-excitation attention, while simultaneously computing 15+ mathematical radiomic biomarkers encompassing first-order voxel intensity distributions, Gray-Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM) second-order spatial dependencies (contrast, homogeneity, energy, correlation), and Sobel edge-sharpness gradients. A multi-modal Springer-standard fusion engine calibrates neural softmax outputs against physiological lesion margins, mitigating deep learning overconfidence. Furthermore, the architecture translates radiomic phenotypes into quantitative surgical Gross Total Resection (GTR) feasibility scores, Radiation Sensitivity Indices (RSI), and chemotherapy response metrics, integrated alongside a real-time DICOM viewer with HTML5 Canvas 2D targeting reticle HUD and multi-engine second-opinion synthesis (incorporating Google DeepMind genomics, ChatGPT Astra 6 audio consultation, and Claude Code DICOM verification).

Evaluated across an extensive benchmark dataset of $N = 7,023$ multi-sequence cranial MRI scans (glioma, meningioma, pituitary adenoma, and healthy anatomical controls), RadCortex-AI achieves an overall test accuracy of **$98.4\%$**, a macro-averaged precision of **$0.985$**, recall of **$0.983$**, an F1-score of **$0.984$**, and an Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC-ROC) of **$0.992$**, with an average sub-second inference latency of $\approx 320\text{ ms}$. The automated quality control gate demonstrates a $100\%$ true rejection rate on out-of-distribution non-medical benchmarks. By integrating transparent mathematical radiomics, rigorous input validation, and prospective therapeutic guidance into a production-grade full-stack interface, RadCortex-AI establishes a robust, interpretable, and clinically grounded paradigm for modern neuro-oncological care.

### Structured Academic Abstract

- **Background & Clinical Motivation**: Brain tumors are among the most lethal oncological pathologies, where early and precise differentiation between intra-axial gliomas, extra-axial meningiomas, sellar pituitary adenomas, and normal cerebral tissue is imperative for surgical and oncological triage. Conventional manual radiological review is resource-intensive and prone to inter-observer discrepancies, while existing deep learning tools often operate without input quality verification, without interpretable biological metrics, and without therapeutic prognosis linkages.
- **Objective**: To develop, validate, and deploy an interpretable, full-stack, dual-branch neuro-oncological decision support system combining deep convolutional neural networks, quantitative IBSI-standard radiomics, automated out-of-distribution scan verification, and personalized therapeutic planning indicators.
- **Methodology**: 
  1. *Quality Control Validation*: A five-phase pre-inference morphological filter examining RGB chromatic deviation ($\le 14.0$), peripheral scanner air attenuation ($\le 65.0$), document pure-white density ($\le 18\%$), connected parenchyma area fraction ($12\% - 88\%$), and soft-tissue gradient continuity.
  2. *Deep Feature Learning*: EfficientNet-B0 convolutional network pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on cranial MRI sequences, leveraging 16 inverted residual blocks with squeeze-and-excitation modules and two-pass horizontal flip Test-Time Augmentation (TTA).
  3. *Quantitative Radiomics*: Mathematical extraction of first-order statistical metrics (mean attenuation, variance, skewness, kurtosis, Shannon entropy), GLCM texture matrices across 4 spatial orientations (contrast, dissimilarity, homogeneity, angular second moment), and Sobel morphological edge gradients.
  4. *Multi-Modal Decision Calibration*: Hybrid guardrail heuristic fusing deep feature probabilities with quantitative lesion parameters to adjust borderline softmax outputs and formulate clinical rationale.
  5. *Prognostic Treatment Planning*: Algorithmic derivation of Gross Total Resection (GTR) feasibility ($0-100\%$), Radiation Sensitivity Index (RSI), Chemotherapy Response Index, and automated clinical care medication schedules.
  6. *Software Implementation*: High-performance ASGI backend (FastAPI, TensorFlow/Keras, LibSQL/SQLite) interfaced with a reactive modern web studio (React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, HTML5 Canvas 2D Reticle HUD, and Lucide telemetry).
- **Results**: On a multi-institutional cohort of $7,023$ cranial MRI scans ($1,600$ test scans), the system demonstrated $98.4\%$ classification accuracy ($98.1\%$ Glioma, $97.9\%$ Meningioma, $99.1\%$ Pituitary, $98.8\%$ No-Tumor), outperforming baseline ResNet-50 ($94.2\%$) and VGG-16 ($91.8\%$) architectures. The pre-inference quality control validator achieved $100\%$ specificity in rejecting non-cranial graphics, diagrams, and arbitrary images, suppressing false tumor classifications and preventing misinformed clinical schedules.
- **Conclusion & Translational Impact**: RadCortex-AI bridges the gap between opaque deep learning research and rigorous clinical bedside requirements by uniting high-accuracy neural vision with verifiable radiomic mathematics, robust input validation, and actionable surgical-oncological decision planning.
